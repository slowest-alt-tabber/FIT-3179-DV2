03 — The student experience

The Student Experience Survey reports indicators such as teaching quality, peer engagement, learning resources and student support. University-level ratings provide context for the completion results, but they do not track the same individuals in the completion cohorts. Differences between these measures should not be interpreted as evidence that student experience causes course completion.

About the data

Completion rates follow commencing student cohorts over specified periods. Student Experience Survey indicators describe reported experiences among surveyed students. The two sources do not automatically track the same individuals.

Any relationship between completion rates and survey indicators should be interpreted as an association, not proof of a causal effect.
