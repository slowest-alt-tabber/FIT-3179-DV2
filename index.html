From commencement to completion

01 / Completion over time

Completion grows with longer follow-up

Follow-up duration changes the share recorded as completed.

Completion across follow-up periods

Completion rises as cohorts receive longer follow-up.

Part-time study, interruptions and later returns can extend the graduation timeline.

Domestic student outcomes after nine years

Completion is the largest outcome after nine years.

Study load, financial pressure and course fit may shape different student pathways.

02 / Study pathways and student groups

Completion differs across pathways

Study field, entry preparation and student background align with different completion outcomes.

Nine-year completion by study field

Nine-year completion varies across fields of education.

Course structures, placements and student backgrounds may contribute to these differences.

Completion outcomes by university entry rank

Higher entry-rank bands generally show stronger completion.

Prior preparation and academic support may shape students' early progress.

Completion rates across student groups

The displayed 2021 completion rates range from 50% to 71.7%.

Financial pressure, access barriers and culturally safe support may shape study continuity.

03 / Student experience

Experience varies across students

Study level, study field and provider relate to different student experiences.

Undergraduate and postgraduate ratings

Postgraduates rate most measures higher; peer engagement remains comparatively low.

Study schedules and work commitments may affect campus and peer involvement.

Provider ratings in 2017 and 2025

Provider ratings changed in different directions between 2017 and 2025.

Teaching changes, student mix and local resources may contribute to variation.

Experience ratings by study area

Ratings vary by study area, with peer engagement often weaker.

Course design and opportunities for group work may shape student responses.

How experience measures change rank

Skills development and learning resources usually rank above peer engagement.

Students can access learning materials independently, while peer contact requires coordination.

04 / Place and support

Location adds context

Support ratings and disability completion show distinct patterns across states and campuses.

Student support ratings across states

Student support ratings vary across states and sampled providers.

Provider coverage and local service access may shape these patterns.

Student support at sampled campuses

Support ratings differ across sampled campus locations.

Staff capacity, service design and student needs may vary between campuses.

Disability completion across states

Completion rates range from 48.7% in Queensland to 63.5% in Victoria.

These differences highlight areas for further investigation into accessibility and student support.
